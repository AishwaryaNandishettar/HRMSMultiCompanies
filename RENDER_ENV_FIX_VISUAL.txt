Q: Why not use my Gmail address?
A: ❌ Gmail addresses don't work with Resend API!
   ❌ Gmail requires SMTP configuration
   ❌ You don't want to use Gmail anyway (that's why we switched!)

Q: Why onboarding@resend.dev?
A: ✅ It's Resend's official test email address
   ✅ Works immediately without domain verification
   ✅ Perfect for testing and development
   ✅ Free to use

Q: Can I use my own domain email?
A: ✅ Yes, but AFTER you verify your domain with Resend!
   1. Go to Resend Dashboard → Domains
   2. Add your domain (e.g., omoikaneinnovations.com)
   3. Add DNS records (SPF, DKIM, Return-Path)
   4. Wait for verification
   5. Then you can use: noreply@omoikaneinnovations.com
